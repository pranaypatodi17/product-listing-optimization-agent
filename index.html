    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Will Listwise publish changes without my approval?

    Never by default. Every rewrite goes to an approval queue. You can enable auto-publish per listing for low-risk updates.

    Which marketplaces are supported?

    Amazon (US, UK, EU, IN), Shopify, WooCommerce and CSV import for everything else.

    How does it use customer reviews?

    Reviews are clustered into themes — complaints, praise and unanswered questions — and turned into copy fixes, FAQ entries and product feedback.

    Is the generated copy policy-compliant?

    Yes. Rewrites are checked against marketplace style rules, banned claims and character limits before they reach you.

    Can agencies manage multiple clients?

    The Scale plan includes unlimited workspaces, white-label reports and role-based access.
